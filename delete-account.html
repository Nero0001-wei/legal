Delete Your Echo Account

Effective date: June 3, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Echo account and associated personal data. Echo is provided by YONGWEI RUAN.

Deleting your Echo account is permanent. It does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription. If you have Echo Pro, cancel the subscription through your Apple Account subscription settings.

Delete from inside Echo

  1. Open Echo.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Open the Legal section.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Read the confirmation message and confirm deletion.

What deletion removes

Account deletion removes your Echo account and associated personal data from active Echo records, including saved thoughts, private images, private audio, profile information, AI job records, usage records, and entitlement records linked to your Echo account, except where limited retention is required for legal, billing, fraud-prevention, security, or dispute-resolution reasons.

Subscriptions

App Store subscriptions are managed by Apple. Deleting your Echo account does not cancel, refund, pause, or change an Apple subscription. To manage or cancel a subscription, open your Apple Account subscription settings or visit https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
